Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 23 October 2026
Tagline: No cloud upload, no contact sharing. Built for POPIA.

Core Principle: All processing is 100% on-device. We do NOT collect, store, transmit, upload, or share your contacts, call logs, or phone numbers to any server, cloud, or third party.

1. Who We Are

CallGuard is operated by Toyz Auto / R Studios Online.

2. What We Process (On-Device Only)

  • Incoming caller ID (temporary, checked against local blocklist)
  • User-created blocklist (stored locally in app sandbox)
  • We do NOT process: contacts, address book, names, emails, location

3. Legal Basis Under POPIA

Consent (Section 11) via in-app consent screen, and legitimate interest to protect you from spam/harassment. Purpose is specific: spam call detection and blocking on your device only.

4. No Third Party Sharing

We share nothing with anyone. No advertisers, no data brokers, no analytics that upload contacts.

5. Data Retention & Security (POPIA Section 19)

Data remains on your device until you delete app or clear blocklist. On-device encryption via iOS/Android sandbox, no network transmission of personal data.

6. Your Rights (POPIA Section 23-25)

You may access your local blocklist in-app, delete by clearing blocklist or uninstalling, object by disabling call blocking in Settings > Phone > Call Blocking, and lodge complaint with Information Regulator at inforegulator.org.za

7. Information Officer

Registered with Information Regulator as required by POPIA Section 55-56.
